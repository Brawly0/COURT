## The numbers that decide the gate

- Minutes of **unprompted** argument about who did it (not rules questions): ______
  - *Pass bar: 15+*
- Audible reactions at the reveal (count gasps, shouts, laughter): ______
- Verdict delivered: GUILTY / NOT GUILTY / MISTRIAL — and was it **correct**? ______
- Did anyone say "one more case" without being asked? YES / NO

## Texture (what the numbers miss)

- Best quote of the session:
- Which witness did they trust that they shouldn't have?
- What did they never find? (from the proof chain / ledger)
- Longest dead-air moment and when it happened:
- Did the defendant's fragments create a decision (tell / hide / lie)? What did they do?
- Did anyone try the bribe rule? What happened?

## Verdict on the format

- What dragged?
- What confused people about the rules (not the case)?
- If you could change ONE number (timer, exhibit cap, witness count), what and why?
